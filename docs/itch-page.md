## Settings on the upload

- Kind of project: HTML. Tick "This file will be played in the browser".
- Embed size: 1280 × 800, with "Mobile friendly" ticked (portrait) and the fullscreen button on.
- Saves use the browser's local storage, so they stay with the browser on that device. Export and Import in the menu move a save between devices.

## Title

Undersong

## Short description

Dig toward a song under the mountain.

## Description

The village of Holloway digs. Under the topsoil there is copper, under the roots there are lanterns that forget, under the water there is a town with lit windows that looks a lot like home. Somewhere below all of it, something is singing.

Undersong is an incremental mining game in side-on pixel cross-section. Tap or drag to dig, hire miners, light the dark, haul ore up the shaft and smelt it into better picks. When the village can go no further, let the mountain fall: the Cave-in takes the village's memory but leaves you Echoes and every verse you found, and the next cycle digs faster.

- Four acts and twelve verses, from the topsoil to the Hollow Heart.
- Idle and active: the village keeps digging while you're away.
- Helpers take over each chore soon after it appears.
- Two endings, Endless Depth, and New Song+.
- Eighteen achievements. Settings for motion, text size and shape marks.

## Controls

Tap rock to dig it. Drag from rock to dig a path (on touch, press and hold first). Drag empty space, scroll, or use WASD to look around.
